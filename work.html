Philosophy

Our view on AI use.

We believe in the immense power of artificial intelligence to accelerate workflows, generate rapid boilerplate, and help us explore diverse UI/UX design variations at scale. However, we hold a firm line: the soul of a product is inherently human.


While AI acts as a highly capable brush, the vision, the empathy for the end-user, and the core creative ideas must always originate from human minds. The major contributor is, and always will be, a human. We leverage AI to build faster, but human intuition strictly dictates what we build and why.
