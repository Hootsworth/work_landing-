Your cursor,upgraded toan AI agent.

Wiggle is a Chrome extension that turns your mouse into an AI agent. It sees what you see, clicks through the page, fills in forms and rewrites what’s on screen. No more tab-switching, no more copy-pasting.

Install WiggleSee it work
123 AI Mode: on
Ctrl/Cmd K deploys the autonomous agent
Ctrl/Cmd Shift L turns on AI selection mode
Runs on your own Gemini API key

The modern web is fragmented. Deep research means ten tabs. Complex forms are tedious. Summarising a two-hour video means leaving the page. Wiggle collapses all of it into one panel that floats above your browser.

Point at the web. Change it.

Click anything, rewrite it

Turn on AI Mode, click any text and ask Wiggle to rewrite it. The new words flow into the live page one by one.

Sniper vision picks the target

Hit Ctrl/Cmd + K. Wiggle puts a numbered badge on every interactive element, takes a snapshot, and decides what to click.

Ghost auto-fill

Type /autofill and Wiggle fills the whole form at once, using your long-term memory profile.

Inject new UI

Ask for a dark mode toggle or a sticky note. Wiggle writes the code and grafts it into the page you’re on.

Set it up in three steps.

  1. 1Load the extensionOpen chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select the extension folder from the repo.
  2. 2Add your brainClick the Wiggle icon in your toolbar and paste your Gemini API key.
  3. 3EngageCtrl/Cmd Shift L for AI selection mode. Ctrl/Cmd K to deploy the agent.
