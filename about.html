Who we are

Rebelling against the
boring web.

We are a tight-knit team of designers, engineers, and motion obsessives. We believe that interacting with a screen should feel just as satisfying as popping bubble wrap.

The internet got too stiff.

Somewhere along the line, web design became an exercise in filling rigid boxes with perfectly aligned text. Everything became a template. Everything stopped reacting to the user.

Singulr was founded to bring physics back to the screen.

We build digital experiences that stretch, bounce, and follow you around. We engineer interactions that make your users smile, because memorable brands aren't built on grids—they're built on feeling.

Our DNA.

👆

Tactile

Buttons should feel magnetic. Menus should snap. We design interfaces that give the user immediate, satisfying feedback with every click and hover.

🎾

Bouncy

We reject linear animations. Using advanced cubic-bezier curves and spring physics, we ensure that our digital products feel organically alive.

🧠

Human

Technology should adapt to people, not the other way around. We build intuitive flows that require zero cognitive load to navigate.
