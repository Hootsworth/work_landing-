The short version

  • This website has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers.
  • Our tools do their work on your device wherever they can. We don’t sell data and we don’t run ads.
  • Wavvy keeps your Wi-Fi login details in your browser profile. They are stored unencrypted and never synced.
  • Wiggle runs on your own Gemini API key, so the page content it works with can go to Google.
  • Our open source code is public, so you can check any of this yourself.

Who we are

Singulr Tech (“we”, “us”) builds automation and AI products. If you have a question about this policy or your data, write to hello@singulr.tech.

This website

  • We don’t ask you to create an account, and we don’t use cookies for analytics or advertising.
  • Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from cdnjs (Cloudflare). When your browser fetches them, those providers receive your IP address and standard request details, under their own privacy policies.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, pages requested and time. We use them to keep the site secure and running, not to profile you.
  • Our forms (for example, the careers application) open your email app with a message filled in. We only receive what you choose to send. If you email us, we use your message and address to reply.

Our software

Each project handles different data. Here is what we say about each one, based on how it works today. The code is open, so the repository is the final word.

Wavvy

Wavvy needs your Wi-Fi username and password to log into portals for you. You enter them in the extension’s settings. They are stored unencrypted in your browser profile, are never synced to other devices, and stay in your browser. Wavvy checks your connection every minute and when your laptop wakes, so it can notice a login portal. See our post on what Wavvy stores.

Wiggle

Wiggle runs on your own Google Gemini API key, which you paste into the extension. When you ask Wiggle to act on a page, the content it works with can be sent from your browser to Google’s Gemini API, under Google’s terms and privacy policy. We don’t receive it.

LinkUp

LinkUp connects two devices directly, with end-to-end encryption. Your screen, keyboard, mouse, clipboard and files travel peer to peer, not through our servers. On the same network, devices pair over a local connection. Across networks, signaling helps the two devices find each other before the direct link takes over.

Shiny Fishstick

Shiny Fishstick crawls the sites you point it at and generates files for you. Those files are yours.

XRisk

XRisk runs wherever you run it, and is built to keep its audit trail and telemetry local. Whether anything leaves your machine is up to your configuration.

Projects that aren’t open source

Some of what we build is closed. Those projects will say what data they handle before you install them.

What we do with data

  • We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.
  • We use what you send us to reply to you, fix bugs and review applications.
  • We share it only with service providers who help us run the site and our email, or when the law requires it.
  • We keep job applications while we’re considering them, and delete them if you ask.

Your choices

  • Removing one of our browser extensions removes the data it stored in your browser.
  • You can email us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under local law. We’ll honour valid requests.

Children

Our site and products aren’t aimed at children under 13, or older where local law says so. We don’t knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

We try to keep data safe, but no system is perfect. Our security page lists known limits, including how Wavvy stores details, and explains how to report a vulnerability.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we’ll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll say so on the blog.

Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: hello@singulr.tech.
