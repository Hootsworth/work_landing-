Most languages were designed for deterministic inputs. Agents live with the opposite: unreliable pages, rate limits, bot detection and probabilistic model output. RexScript makes that the default assumption, not an afterthought.

What it’s built around.

Goal-first

You describe what you want to accomplish. Recovery and fallbacks are part of the language, not try/catch noise scattered through your code.

Web-native

Observing, finding and synthesising are keywords, not library calls, and confidence is part of the picture from the start.

Policy-aware

Every action is checked before and after it runs, and leaves an auditable trace behind.

Bridged

Drop into other runtimes when the task calls for it, without leaving the agent’s execution context.
